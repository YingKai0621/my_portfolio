# Creative direction

**Quiet Precision. Bold Curiosity.** / **Tỉ mỉ trong từng chi tiết. Táo bạo trong khám phá.**

Glacial Precision: cinematic editorial with measured playful moments, inspired by black basalt and glacial blue light. The person is cheerful, gentle, calm under pressure, deadline-conscious, focused and meticulous; loves discovering new technology, programming and creating useful things. Career goal is deep technical expertise in software engineering. The site should demonstrate care, not merely claim it.

Visual narrative: a structured digital landscape opens with responsive geometry, then a warm human introduction, rigorous engineering evidence, deeper research, and a playful but restrained closing. Use dark muted surfaces, restrained Glacier Blue (#1BA6CD) accents, Bone (#E8F3F6) type on Black Basalt (#0B1013), editorial asymmetry and ample breathing room. Avoid neon overload, excessive glassmorphism, default SaaS template aesthetics, fake terminal theatrics, gratuitous gamification and generic AI imagery.

Hero (P2 V3, pending visual approval): a personal editorial poster. The uppercase name is the dominant canvas-wide typographic element. The portrait layer is intentionally empty until the owner supplies an approved transparent portrait; the faceless silhouette is rejected and must not appear as a final asset. Verified professional roles sit at lower left; real GitHub and LinkedIn links sit at lower right. The approved official Isometric Cubes geometry fills the background as a quiet secondary layer. Do not reintroduce the encrypted name, rotating slogan, unavailable CTAs, or visible dock while later sections do not exist.

H2.1 established the first-viewport baseline, not a deletion of P2: Glacial Awakening opens with Duy's server-rendered name and verified specialization beside a low-angle, close-cropped mecha. Graphite-silver armor, cold directional reflections, a subtle dither/atmosphere field and oversized outline lettering create cinematic depth without MACACO's branding or HUD. The owner, not the character, remains the principal identity. P2 poster/cubes and the H2.1 component stay in source until H2 receives visual approval; the former P7 section is temporarily unmounted. The provisional character remains development-only pending written permission for public distribution.

H2.2/H2.3 extends that opening into **one continuous Hero–About cinematic** rather than two disconnected sections: identity close-up, skeletal awakening, a wider iridescent build performance, a descending character beside the verified personal introduction, and a restrained transition from Black Basalt into Bone. The model exits as the normal-flow P3 About composition takes over; its cards and experience pillars are preserved. Scale, shot angle, material response, light and typography change together, but Duy's identity remains the narrative subject. The empty left side of the final light chapter is reserved for an approved real portrait; no generic or synthetic portrait is supplied. Desktop pinning is bounded, mobile remains naturally scrollable, and the local provisional asset never enters a public build.

H2.4 visual refinement supersedes the *visual* treatment of that sequence, not its architecture: the opening now has a local Deep Void/Gunmetal/Electric Cyan grade, darker per-material armor with narrow specular planes, a spectral transformation, and an original architectural Digital World. The fourth chapter uses a concise systems-to-people bridge instead of repeating P3's BEHIND THE CODE heading and introduction; the fifth uses a short handoff rather than reprinting P3's personality note. About itself remains unchanged. Held camera compositions, clip anticipation and a ~6.2-viewport scroll distance give the character more weight. Violet/pink remain confined to transformation; the site-wide palette is unchanged.

H2.6 supersedes the H2.4/H2.5 **separate About handoff**: the personal introduction, playful note, Explorer/Engineer/Builder identity and three verified experience pillars now unfold in the same dark architectural world after the character's landing. The standalone Bone About is no longer rendered on the homepage. A close helmet, full-body spectral passage, extreme shoulder detail, gesture-linked architecture, root leap/impact and the landed figure beside the owner's actual copy form one directed sequence. Cyan/violet/pink/green are localized cinematic spectral accents, not a change to the site's core palette. The final frame releases directly into Selected Works. The mecha remains a provisional local-only stand-in for a licensed final character; owner visual approval and real-device motion quality remain open. See `H2_6_CINEMATIC_STORYBOARD.md`.

Rhythm: high visual impact through one connected Hero-and-About opening, then strong project imagery; a quiet technical/research field; P6's mechanical chapter-opening threshold and editorial journey; then a strong closing in a later phase. The former P3 cards remain recoverable in source but are not mounted in H2.6. P7 is suspended rather than repeated after P6. One dominant animation per viewport. The P2 poster remains a reversible source fallback. P6 uses an original zipper interaction, not copied Mystery Box source. H2 uses a provisional, replaceable character for local review while keeping camera, lighting, shader and scroll work original; confirm rights before public release. Use original visuals, not game IP or inaccessible paid source.

The three core colors are owner-selected. Supporting tokens, layout and art direction details are provisional and will be reviewed on browser screenshots at P2 before expanding the whole design.
